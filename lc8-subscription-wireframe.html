S4 · FAQ

Common questions

What happens to my credits if I don't use them?

On Pro and Producer plans, unused credits roll over every month and never expire. You keep everything you've paid for.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any time and your plan stays active until the end of your billing period. Any samples you've downloaded are yours to keep.

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?

Yes — we ask for a card upfront so you don't have to re-enter details when your trial ends. You won't be charged anything today. Cancel before day 14 and you'll never pay a penny.

What DAWs does Loopcloud work with?

Loopcloud works with all major DAWs — Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Bitwig, Reaper, and more, as a VST/AU plugin.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Are the samples royalty-free?

All samples downloaded with credits are 100% royalty-free for personal and commercial use. The Producer plan includes an extended commercial licence.
