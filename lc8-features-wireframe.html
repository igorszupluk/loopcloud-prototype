New in Loopcloud 8
Listen

Drop it on your master.
Loopcloud shows you
what fits.

Listen is a VST plugin you drop on any channel in your DAW — typically the master. It reads what your track sounds like and sends that context to Loopcloud, which surfaces loops that actually complement your mix. No more guessing. No more mismatched samples.

✓
Works inside any DAW — Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, and more
✓
Results update as your mix evolves — always relevant to what you've built
✓
Opens a dedicated results tab in Loopcloud — drag and drop straight into your session
✓
Included free with all Loopcloud plans

New in Loopcloud 8
Mobile App

Your library travels
with you. Finish on
desktop.

The Loopcloud mobile app is built for the moments between sessions — browsing sounds on the commute, reviewing your Jams before a studio day, saving a loop idea before you forget it. When you sit back down at your DAW, everything's already there.

✓
Browse and save from the full Loopcloud catalogue on iOS and Android
✓
Your Inspire Jams sync automatically — open them straight on desktop
✓
Save sounds to your library wherever you are — they'll be waiting in your DAW
What it's not
The mobile app is a discovery and sync companion — not a production tool. For the full Loopcloud experience with DAW integration, Studio editing, and plugin access, Loopcloud lives on desktop.
