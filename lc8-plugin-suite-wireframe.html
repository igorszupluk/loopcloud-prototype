S2 · DRUM Plugin
DR
Loopcloud DRUM

Your kit.
Built in seconds.

A 16-pad drum machine that builds unique kits from your sample library — and Loopcloud's 5 million+ catalogue — with a single click. Pattern, sequence, export. Done.

Screenshot placeholder

Loopcloud DRUM UI: full-width view of the 16-pad grid, kit generator button prominent, waveform previews on each pad, per-pad parameter controls (pitch, volume, pan, filter) visible. Dark UI, yellow accents. High-res screenshot from the actual plugin at 1440px width.

Screenshot placeholder

Kit Generator in action — 16 pads filled, "Generate Kit" button highlighted. Shows real sample names loaded per pad.

Kit Generator

A new kit with every click.

Hit "Generate Kit" and 16 pads are instantly filled with samples that fit together. Lock the pads you like, regenerate the rest. It's the fastest way to get from zero to a complete drum kit — using any sample, from any source.

Screenshot placeholder

DRUM sequencer view: step-sequencer grid with an active 4-bar pattern, play/stop transport, tempo field.

Sequencer

Trigger, sequence, or play — your way.

Build patterns in DRUM's built-in sequencer or play pads live via MIDI. When you're happy, export the audio or MIDI directly to your DAW. The patterns you create accumulate into a personal loop library that grows with every session.

Video placeholder
▶

DRUM workflow demo — 60 to 90 second video showing the full workflow: generate kit → lock pads → tweak parameters → export to Ableton. Sourced from the official Loopcloud YouTube channel (V4cUaP59iNs).

Sound Shaping

Mix as you go. Export when it's right.

Each pad has its own pitch, volume, pan, and filter controls — so you're shaping your kit as you build it, not after. Multi-output routing lets advanced users send individual pads to separate DAW channels for total mix control.

🎯

Works with any sample

Import from Loopcloud's 5M+ catalogue or use your own samples. DRUM doesn't care where the sound comes from.

🔁

Audio + MIDI export

Export your pattern as audio or MIDI into any DAW. Drag and drop — the sound is yours to keep.

🎛️

Multi-output routing

Route individual pads to separate mixer channels for surgical control over your kit in the mix.

Curated Sound Library

Sounds from the people who know them.

Every preset in PLAY was built by seasoned sound designers and artists — the same people behind Loopmasters' most acclaimed sample packs. The result is an instrument library that actually sounds like music, not a demo reel.

Playable Macros

Complex sounds. Simple controls.

PLAY's interface is intentionally stripped back. Every instrument exposes just the controls that matter — macro knobs mapped to the most expressive parameters. Automate them in your DAW to add movement and life to any sound.
