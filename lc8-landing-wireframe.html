S3 · Workflow
How it works

Preview, edit, and drop sounds
without leaving your DAW.

01 — Search

Filter by genre, key, BPM, mood

5 million+ samples across every genre. Smart filters surface exactly what fits your session — no rabbit holes.

02 — Preview

Hear it in sync with your project

Loopcloud locks to your DAW's tempo and key. Every sample plays in time, every time — no manual tuning.

03 — Use

Drag and drop. You're done.

Works with Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Cubase and more. Drop it in, keep making music.

04 — Take it anywhere

Find sounds on the go.

Browse and bookmark samples from your phone. Everything syncs to your desktop session — so your ideas are waiting when you sit down to produce.

S3b · Deep Features
Catalogue

A world of sound.

The world's most extensive sample library — millions of loops, one-shots, and stems across every genre, with thousands of new sounds added every week. Whatever style you're chasing, it's already here.

Loopcloud Studio

Make every sound yours.

Pitch it, chop it, reverse it, layer it. Loopcloud Studio gives you real editing tools directly in the app — before anything touches your DAW. Sculpt the perfect sound, then drag it straight in.

Beyond Samples

More than a sample library.

250,000+ MIDI files. 7,000+ synth presets. Whether you need groove inspiration or a bassline starting point, everything lives in one place — alongside your sounds, not buried in a separate app.

Smart Library

Your whole library, finally organized.

Auto-tagging scans and imports your existing samples from any provider — so everything lives in one place, searchable, tagged, and ready. No more hunting through folders.
